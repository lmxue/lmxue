I’m an Assistant Professor at Nanjing University. Previously, I worked as a Postdoc Fellow at [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://hkust.edu.hk/), working with Prof. [Yike Guo](https://cse.hkust.edu.hk/admin/people/faculty/profile/yikeguo) and Prof. [Wei Xue](http://wei-xue.com/). Before that, I worked as a Postdoc Fellow at  [Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen](https://www.cuhk.edu.cn/en), working with Prof. [Haizhou Li](https://colips.org/~eleliha/) and Prof. [Zhizheng Wu](https://drwuz.com/).  I am a co-founder of [Amphion](https://github.com/open-mmlab/Amphion), an open-source platform for Audio, Music, and Speech Generation. 

I received my Ph.D. degree from the Audio, Speech and Language Processing Laboratory at Northwestern Polytechnical University ([ASLP@NWPU](http://www.npu-aslp.org/english)), Xi'an, China, supervised by Prof. [Lei Xie](http://www.nwpu-aslp.org/lxie/). During my Ph.D. study, I performed research at Microsoft, Tencent AI Lab and JD AI Lab. 

My research interests include audio, music, and speech understanding and generation.
